# Licensing guide

This repository uses **open-source code, noncommercial shared content, and
independently protected brand identifiers**, alongside third-party material.
These parts do not all use the same license.
The full license texts and the asset-specific provenance records control if
this guide and a license text ever differ.

## 1. Software code — AGPL-3.0-only

Unless a file says otherwise, the software code in this repository is
licensed under the [GNU Affero General Public License version 3](LICENSE),
SPDX identifier `AGPL-3.0-only`.

This includes TypeScript, JavaScript, CSS, build and validation scripts,
tests, configuration, and the GitHub Actions workflow. A modified version
that users interact with over a computer network must offer those users its
Corresponding Source as required by section 13 of the AGPL.

AGPL-3.0 is an open-source software license and permits commercial use of the
software. Commercial operation does not remove its source-sharing,
attribution, notice, and copyleft obligations.

Copyright © 2026 Leon Made This contributors.

## 2. Original museum content — CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

To the extent that the project owner or an identified contributor holds
copyright or similar rights, the following original content is licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International](LICENSES/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.txt):

- child-facing introductions, narration scripts, parent guides, and other
  original editorial prose;
- project-generated English and Mandarin narration recordings;
- project-generated landscape and portrait exhibit backgrounds;
- original documentation prose, illustrations, screenshots, and editorial
  graphics, except for Brand Assets described below.

Commercial use of this Original Museum Content is not granted. Separate
permission from its respective rights holder is required for commercial use.
Required contributor attribution must be retained.

Facts, ideas, scientific references, and material that is not protected by
copyright are not made exclusive by this notice. Where a source file combines
software structure with editorial prose, the software structure is covered by
the AGPL and the original prose is covered by CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

## 3. Contributions — copyright retained, no default assignment

Contributors retain copyright in their original contributions. Submitting a
contribution does not transfer its copyright to the project owner. By
intentionally submitting material for inclusion, a contributor agrees to the
applicable outbound license: code under AGPL-3.0-only, Original Museum Content
under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, and third-party material under its existing terms.

The project owner receives no separate relicensing grant merely because a
contribution was submitted. Without the contributor's separate agreement, the
project owner cannot relicense that code contribution under a proprietary or
otherwise incompatible license, or offer that contributor's Original Museum
Content under a separate commercial license. Contributors remain free to
license their own work elsewhere. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 4. Brand Assets — independently protected

No license in this repository grants trademark, passing-off, publicity, or
branding rights in the Brand Assets. See [BRAND_POLICY.md](BRAND_POLICY.md).
Attribution and accurate referential use required by another license are not
restricted by this brand reservation.

This reservation prevents confusion about the official source. It does not
prevent a renamed and rebranded fork, modification, redistribution, or other
development permitted by the applicable software and content licenses.

## 5. Third-party and mixed assets — their recorded terms

Third-party libraries, fonts, 3D models, and other external material retain
their own licenses. Model GLBs and the project modifications distributed with
them remain under their recorded CC BY 4.0 terms. Composite poster and
thumbnail images that include those models retain the asset-specific terms
recorded in their provenance.

The applicable source, author, modifications, license, and evidence for every
runtime animal asset are recorded in
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and the corresponding
`src/content/animals/*/provenance/LICENSES/` directory. Every contribution
containing third-party animal material must preserve its author, source,
license, redistribution evidence, and modification record. Nothing in the
AGPL, the content license, or the brand policy replaces or narrows rights
already granted by a third-party licensor.
