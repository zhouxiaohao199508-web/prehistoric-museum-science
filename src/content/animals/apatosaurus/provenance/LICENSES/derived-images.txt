Poster, preview and thumbnail derivation record
===============================================

Transparent poster pair generated: 2026-08-05
Responsive previews and thumbnail refreshed: 2026-08-09

The transparent poster pair remains the accepted deterministic first frame of
the replacement Apatosaurus, with transparent pixels outside the model and no
text, controls, logos, or watermarks.

The six shared responsive model previews and their manifest were regenerated
after retuning the live presentation against the bright high-horizon habitat.
The contact shadow uses a 0.38 horizontal scale, 0.9 depth scale, -0.62
horizontal offset, and 0.56 opacity. Camera-relative light scale is 1.05,
tone-mapping exposure is 1.28, and the landscape and portrait vertical offsets
are 0.035 and 0.05. The animal geometry remains grounded at y=0; these changes
strengthen the four-foot contact cue without changing the GLB.

The collection thumbnail was refreshed after the habitat ground was changed
from a flat gray-green field to natural compact woodland loam with broad moss
variation. The deterministic first animation frame was rendered in the 1440 x
900 production viewer with interface chrome hidden. Pixel comparison against
the same frame with the WebGL canvas hidden located the model, and a 764 x 764
crop at x=549,y=49 was resized to 320 x 320 and encoded as WebP at quality 82.

The model attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms in model-license.txt continue to
apply to all derived images. The project background retains its separately
recorded terms.
