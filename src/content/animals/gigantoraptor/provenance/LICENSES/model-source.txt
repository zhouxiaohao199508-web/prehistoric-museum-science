## Gigantoraptor

- Creator: [seth the yutyrannus](https://sketchfab.com/slang107123456789)
- Source: [gigantoraptor](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/gigantoraptor-e51509d66d464104aef1b72c298a40cf)
- License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes: downloaded as Sketchfab's converted 1K-texture GLB; zero-weight
  joint indices cleared; unused nodes and data pruned; GLB repacked; a
  project-authored 6.5-second in-place museum Idle was created in Blender 5.2
  with the existing rig; body, tail, and jaw motion were retained at 225% of
  the authored base while head and neck motion were increased to 450% and both
  arms to 500% after two local visual reviews; 29 rotation-only channels were
  injected into the normalized base while the root, hips, and legs remained
  stationary; validator-checked.

Attribution: “gigantoraptor” by seth the yutyrannus, licensed under CC BY 4.0;
converted by Sketchfab, normalized, and given a project-authored in-place Idle
