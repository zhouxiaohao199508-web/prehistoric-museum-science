Background generation record
============================

Tool: OpenAI built-in image_gen
Original compositions generated: 2026-07-29; portrait depth revised 2026-07-31
Clarity edits generated: 2026-08-16
Use: project-specific responsive museum exhibit backgrounds

The accepted landscape and portrait compositions were edited separately. The
2026-08-16 edit preserved the horizon, open staging area and edge framing while
removing excessive soft-focus, hazy gouache blur, oversized paper texture and
flat paper-cut softness. Snow mountains, glacier faces, conifers, rocks, frozen
shrubs, residual snow and dry tundra soil were made legible with a controlled
foreground-to-midground-to-distance progression. Atmospheric softening is
limited to the farthest distance. The image remains a refined educational
illustration rather than a photograph.

Both edits exclude animals, people, text, interface elements, logos,
watermarks, platforms, hard ground seams, artificial depth of field, bloom,
fog veils, high-saturation fantasy colour and repeated vegetation patterns.

The reviewed clarity derivatives were first prepared at 4096 x 2304 and
2304 x 4096. For the runtime package they were downscaled to 2048 x 1152 and
1152 x 2048 with Sharp Lanczos3 resampling, then encoded as WebP at quality 82
and effort 6. Ancillary metadata was removed. The application applies no
background blur or depth-of-field filter.

The exact landscape and portrait edit prompts are retained in
.handoff/mammoth-exhibit-background-clarity-v2-2026-08-16/imagegen-prompts.json
and in the generated asset provenance record.

Public release remains subject to the repository owner's final rights review.
