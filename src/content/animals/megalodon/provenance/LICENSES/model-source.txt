The project retained the skinned hierarchy, applied a desaturated matte
aquatic material, and replaced the mouth-focused source action with an
eight-second in-place swimming Idle. The head chain remains nearly stable,
while a travelling propulsion wave grows from the tail root to the tail tip
and the paired fins make restrained supporting movements.
