The project retained the source hierarchy, applied a desaturated matte aquatic
material, added restrained eye detail, and authored four morph targets. Those
targets relax the original permanently up-curved neck, add a visible
head-to-shoulder neck motion, and move all four flippers independently through
two complete swimming cycles in one eight-second in-place Idle.
