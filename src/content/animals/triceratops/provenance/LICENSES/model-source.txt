assets and must not be published merely because they appear in the review
catalog.

## Tyrannosaurus rex

- Creator: [Marcel Schanz](https://sketchfab.com/mschanz)
- Source:
  [Tyrant King - Tyrannosaurus](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tyrant-king-tyrannosaurus-6465a297fa784598adc49f6e0042d449)
- License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes: downloaded as Sketchfab's converted GLB with 1k textures; PBR
  material data converted where necessary; unused data deduplicated and
  pruned; GLB repacked; a project-authored 13-bone armature and deterministic
  skin weights added in Blender; an eight-second in-place Idle authored and
  validator-checked.

Attribution: “Tyrant King - Tyrannosaurus” by Marcel Schanz, licensed under
CC BY 4.0; converted by Sketchfab, normalized, rigged, weighted, and given an
in-place museum Idle by the Prehistoric Animal Museum project.

## Triceratops

- Creator:
  [wojciechmiedziocha](https://sketchfab.com/wojciechmiedziocha)
- Source:
  [Triceratops dinosaur](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/triceratops-dinosaur-87527079bad44917ab1b98a456b46c7e)
- License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes: downloaded as Sketchfab's converted GLB with 1k textures; unused
  data deduplicated and pruned; GLB repacked; a project-authored 10-bone
  armature and deterministic skin weights added in Blender; an eight-second
  in-place head-and-tail Idle authored with the pelvis/tail-root overlap held
  stationary, normalized to four rotation-only channels, and
  validator-checked.

Attribution: “Triceratops dinosaur” by wojciechmiedziocha, licensed under
CC BY 4.0; converted by Sketchfab, normalized, rigged, weighted, and given an
in-place museum Idle by the Prehistoric Animal Museum project.
