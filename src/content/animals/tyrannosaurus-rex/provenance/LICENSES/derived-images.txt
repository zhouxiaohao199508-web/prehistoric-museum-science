Poster and thumbnail derivation record
======================================

Poster generated: 2026-07-30
Responsive previews and thumbnail refreshed: 2026-08-09

The transparent poster remains the accepted browser-rendered presentation of
the repaired rigged Tyrannosaurus rex. It captures the live Idle with repaired
foreclaws and the controlled two-pulse bite after the jaw, mouth lining, teeth,
tongue, palate, hip seams, and stationary feet were reviewed.

The six shared responsive model previews and their manifest were regenerated
after reducing the camera-relative light scale from 1.3 to 0.95 and setting the
tone-mapping exposure to 1.15 for a softer cloud-filtered presentation. The
live Hell Creek scene also suppresses the two decorative parallel forest
sunbeams while retaining the subtle dust and leaf atmosphere.

The collection thumbnail was refreshed after the bright high-horizon habitat
replacement. The deterministic first animation frame was rendered in the
1440 x 900 production viewer with interface chrome hidden. Pixel comparison
against the same frame with the WebGL canvas hidden located the model, and a
774 x 774 crop at x=537,y=10 was resized to 320 x 320 and encoded as WebP at
quality 82.

The model attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms in model-license.txt continue to
apply. The background was generated specifically for this project and is
recorded separately in background-generation.txt.
