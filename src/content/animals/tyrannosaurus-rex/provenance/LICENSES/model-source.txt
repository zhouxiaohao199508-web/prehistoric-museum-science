## Tyrannosaurus rex

- Creator: [Marcel Schanz](https://sketchfab.com/mschanz)
- Source:
  [Tyrant King - Tyrannosaurus](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tyrant-king-tyrannosaurus-6465a297fa784598adc49f6e0042d449)
- License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes: downloaded as Sketchfab's converted GLB with 1k textures; PBR
  material data converted where necessary; unused data deduplicated and
  pruned; GLB repacked; Body base-colour midtones lifted
  with a 0.80 luminance gamma and maximum 1.50 channel scale after a second
  owner review while other PBR textures remained unchanged; a project-authored
  13-bone armature and deterministic skin weights added in Blender; an
  eight-second in-place Idle authored with head, arm, tail, and lower-jaw
  motion; lower-jaw membership separated from the upper palate after close-up
  review; the integrated outer lower-jaw skin and shared mouth lining were
  reweighted with smooth hinge and mouth-edge transitions so the lower-jaw
  skin, teeth, and tongue follow the lower jaw while upper teeth and palate
  follow the head. Both 33-vertex hip seam rings were matched to the torso
  weights, then each upper leg was blended back to its stationary leg bone over
  0.16 model units so the hips remain closed while the feet stay planted. Both
  detached fingernail components on each forelimb were reassigned to the
  corresponding arm bone. The added opening remains limited to about 4 degrees
  and follows one primary and one smaller secondary opening-and-closing pulse;
  normalized to ten rotation-only channels and validator-checked.

Attribution: “Tyrant King - Tyrannosaurus” by Marcel Schanz, licensed under
CC BY 4.0; converted by Sketchfab, normalized, and given a project-authored
Body base-colour midtone lift, Blender rig, repaired weights, and museum Idle
by the Prehistoric Animal Museum project.
